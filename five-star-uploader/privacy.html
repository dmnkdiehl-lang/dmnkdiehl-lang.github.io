Privacy Policy — Five Star Uploader

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Information We Access

Five Star Uploader only accesses data strictly required to authenticate and publish videos to the connected TikTok account via TikTok's official APIs:

  • Basic Profile Information (user.info.basic): Used to verify the connected TikTok account inside the local dashboard.
  • Content Posting Permissions (video.upload, video.publish): Used exclusively to upload and publish videos explicitly approved by the creator.

2. Local Storage & Data Security

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored strictly locally on the creator's private computer. Five Star Uploader does not operate an external tracking server, does not collect personal data from third parties, and never sells, rents, or shares user data with third parties.

3. Data Retention & Revocation

Tokens are retained locally only for as long as the creator keeps the TikTok account connected. You can disconnect the App at any time by revoking access in your TikTok account settings (Settings & Privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) and deleting the local configuration file.

4. Contact

For privacy inquiries or deletion requests, please contact the operator via GitHub: github.com/dmnkdiehl-lang.
